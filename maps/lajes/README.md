# Lajes — 8/9 production pilot

## Decision

The current map is the playable and visual baseline, not a surface for endless patching. Production will be rebuilt through art direction, a metric kit and Blender assembly while preserving routes and gameplay contracts unless a change is explicitly approved.

“8/9” is the owner's visual target. It can only be declared achieved after reviewing the integrated game, never from concepts or isolated previews.

## Deliverables

- `research/`: classified references and application matrix.
- `concepts/`: direction and three cameras comparable to the baseline.
- Editable Blender sources: stored privately in `corosolto/studio-assets`.
- `gauntlet/`: loops, evidence and decisions.
- `releases/`: versioned candidates for future integration.

## Validation slice

Build and approve this slice before the full map:

1. An alley with two facades and real circulation.
2. A corner with sidewalk, commerce and material junctions.
3. An accessible rooftop with stairs, water tanks and skyline.

## Gates

| Gate | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Research | Building types, streets, openings, roofs, infrastructure and atmosphere | Matrix with source and application |
| G1 Direction | Specific community, kit, palette and composition | Concepts with constructible callouts |
| G2 Greybox | Coherent scale, junctions and circulation | Source scene and fixed cameras |
| G3 Dressing | Identity without uniform noise or random accumulation | A/B evidence and free roam |
| G4 Atmosphere | Sky, light, background and audio belonging to one place | `med` and `low` |
| G5 Gameplay | Routes, shooting, navigation and budgets preserved | Client integration checks |
| G6 Owner | Visual target reviewed with explicit limitations | Recorded decision |

No gate passes on concept art alone. Self-review and independent review are recorded separately.
